Progression

Once a weight feels comfortable for 2–3 weeks, increase by the smallest increment available. Early on, focus on feeling the right muscle working — connection matters more than load.

Fibromyalgia Note

All core exercises are compression-free. On flare days, skip or shorten the core movement. Never push through midsection pain — the rest of the session still counts.
